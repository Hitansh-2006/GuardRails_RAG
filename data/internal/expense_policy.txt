Acme Corp - Expense and Reimbursement Policy (Internal)

Travel: economy class for flights under 6 hours. Hotels up to Rs 6,000 per night in metro cities and Rs 4,000 elsewhere. Book through the approved travel portal where possible.
Meals while travelling: up to Rs 1,200 per day with receipts.
Client entertainment: needs prior written approval from a department head.
Internet and phone: employees on the remote-eligible plan get a Rs 1,000 monthly allowance.

Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense, with original receipts attached in the finance portal. Reimbursement is processed in the next payroll cycle. Claims above Rs 25,000 need director approval.
